# The Ninety-Nine Names of Allah (أسماء الله الحسنى)
### Personal Digital Book, Situational Dhikr Advisor & Email Reminder System
Based strictly on the 1978 edition by **Shems Friedlander** (*Harper Colophon Books*, ISBN 0-06-090621-9, Istanbul Jerrahi Halveti Sufi tradition).

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **📖 Realistic 3D Book Reader**:
   - Dual-page 3D leather hardcover layout with gold foil borders and central spine shadow.
   - 3D page flip navigation (Previous/Next, slider, or keyboard left/right arrows).
   - Mode Toggle: **Authentic Book Scan** (views the real scanned page from `E:\My Drive\99NamesOfAllah`) vs **Enhanced Typography**.
   - **Inspect Full Scan**: One-click zoom and pan modal displaying the raw uncompressed 3154×1956 scan.

2. **🔍 Situational & Prayer Query Engine**:
   - Query by prayer time: *After Fajr (Morning)*, *After Zuhr (Thursday)*, *Blessed Friday (Jumu'ah)*, *Between Maghrib & Isha*, *Night / Tahajjud*, *Fasting / Iftar*.
   - Query by life need: *Anxiety & Heavy Heart*, *Debt & Sustenance (Rizq)*, *Children & Pregnancy*, *Healing & Sickness*, *Protection & Tyranny*.
   - Displays verbatim book prescriptions, formulas (`YA-...`), exact repetition counts, and stated benefits.

3. **📧 Personal Email Reminder System**:
   - **Friday Continuous Wazifa Reminder**: Automated reminder every Friday morning highlighting `AL-BASIR` (100× between Farz & Sunnah for esteem) and `AL-AWWAL` (1,000× for 40 Fridays to have a child or reunite with a traveler).
   - **Daily Post-Fajr Reminder**: Automated reminder every morning at dawn for `AR-RAHIM` (100× for friendly treatment) and `AR-RAHMAN` (100× for sharp memory).
   - **Thursday Post-Zuhr Reminder**: Automated reminder for `AS-SAMI'` (100× in silence for fulfillment of desires).
   - **Instant Test & Preview**: Click *"Trigger Friday Reminder Now"* to test dispatch, with local HTML preview fallback even before entering SMTP credentials!

4. **📿 Built-in Digital Tasbih Counter**:
   - Target count automatically pre-set according to the book's prescription (e.g. 21×, 40×, 70×, 100×, 1,000×, 3,000×).
   - Gentle audio click chime and vibration feedback.
   - Celebration confetti animation upon completing the prescribed count.

5. **📜 Scholarly Validity & Authenticity Analysis**:
   - Transparent 3-tier categorization:
     1. **Divine Names**: 100% Quranic and Sahih Hadith (Mutawatir).
     2. **Repetition Numbers & Recipes**: Classical Sufi *Mujarrabat* (experiential spiritual practices of the righteous predecessors, e.g. Imam Al-Ghazali, Sheikh Abdul Qadir Jilani, Ottoman masters).
     3. **Fidelity**: All texts directly faithful to Shems Friedlander's 1978 scans.

## 🚀 How to Launch

Simply double-click:
```
G:\99NamesOfAllahApp\LaunchApp.bat
```
Or run from terminal:
```bash
cd G:\99NamesOfAllahApp\backend
node server.js
```
Then open your browser to **http://localhost:5000**.
